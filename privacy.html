Knit Assistant Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

Knit Assistant ("the app") is a knitting row tracker for Mac, iPad and iPhone. This policy explains what

information the app handles. The short version: your projects stay on your devices. The app has no accounts, no ads and no tracking, and the developer does not collect or see your patterns or projects. The only thing that can leave your device on its own is optional, anonymous usage counts, and only if you turn them on.

What the app stores

Everything you add is saved on your own device: projects, patterns and rows, ticked rows, counters, notes,

timer sessions, yarn and gauge details, photos, pattern PDFs, and your settings. The app also keeps a few

automatic backups of this data on the same device. If you choose "Back up now", the backup file is saved

wherever you choose. We do not receive any of it.

Features that send data elsewhere (only if you use them)

These are optional. Each one sends data only to the service named, and only when you turn it on or use it.

  • Wi-Fi sync between your devices. Your Mac, iPad and iPhone can sync directly with each other over your

    • local network using a pairing code. This data does not pass through any server of ours.

  • Cloud sync (optional). If you enter your own Supabase project address and key, the app syncs your data to

    • that project. It is your account and your database; the developer cannot see it. Supabase's own privacy

    policy applies to data stored there.

  • Smart import with Claude (optional). If you enter your own Anthropic API key, the pattern PDF or text you

    • choose to import is sent to Anthropic to be read and turned into rows. Your key is stored only on your device.

    Anthropic's privacy policy applies to that data.

  • Ravelry (optional). If you connect Ravelry, you sign in on Ravelry's own site. The app then reads the

    • pattern library information you ask for from Ravelry. Sign-in tokens are stored only on your device. Ravelry's

    privacy policy applies.

  • YouTube videos (optional). If you add a YouTube link to a project, the video plays from YouTube's

    • privacy-enhanced player and its thumbnail is loaded from YouTube. YouTube (Google) may collect information

    about that viewing under its own privacy policy.

Anonymous usage counts (optional)

When the app first has a project, it asks whether you'd like to share anonymous usage counts. The answer is no unless you choose yes, and you can change it at any time in Settings > Help improve Knit Assistant. If you turn it on, the app sends short events to TelemetryDeck, a privacy-focused analytics service, so the developer can see which features are used and where the pattern reader struggles.

  • What is sent: fixed words and counts, such as "imported a PDF", "rows ticked: 6-20", the app version, whether the device is an iPhone, iPad or Mac, the device's language (for example "en"), and roughly how long since the app was first opened. Numbers are sent as ranges.
  • What is never sent: pattern names or text, project names, notes, photos, yarn details, anything you type, your name, email, Apple ID, contacts or location.
  • No tracking: the events are tied to a random number made on your device and scrambled before it leaves. It can't be traced back to you, and turning the setting off deletes it. The data is not used for advertising or shared with advertisers.
  • TelemetryDeck's own privacy policy applies to the events it receives.

Device features and permissions

The app asks for these permissions only for the feature named, and you can turn each off in system settings:

  • Camera: to scan the pairing code that links your devices, and to take photos of your projects.
  • Photos and files: to attach pictures and pattern PDFs you pick.
  • Microphone and speech recognition (iPhone): to count rows hands-free when you say "next" or "back". Audio

    • is used only to recognise those words. Speech recognition runs on your phone where it can; where Apple needs to

    process it, Apple's privacy policy applies. The app does not save recordings.

  • Local network: to sync with your other devices over Wi-Fi.
  • Reading text from photos and on-device AI: photo text reading uses Apple's Vision framework and the

    • optional on-device model uses Apple's on-device language model. Both run on your device.

  • Widgets, Live Activities and Siri/Shortcuts: the row counter on your Home Screen, Lock Screen and in

    • Shortcuts reads a small summary of your current project that is shared between the app and its widget on the

    same device.

What we do not do

We do not sell or share your information, show ads, build advertising profiles, or track you across other apps or websites. The only analytics are the optional anonymous usage counts described above (and Apple's own opt-in App Store and crash statistics, which Apple controls).

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays on your devices until you delete it. You can delete projects and patterns inside the app, and

deleting the app removes its data from that device. Data you sent to a service you chose (Supabase, Anthropic,

Ravelry or YouTube) is kept under that service's own policy, and you can delete it with them.

Security

Data is stored in the app's private storage on your device. Syncing between your devices uses a pairing code,

and optional services are contacted over HTTPS.
